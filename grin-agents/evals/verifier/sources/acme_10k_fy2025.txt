ACME SUPPLY CORPORATION
ANNUAL REPORT ON FORM 10-K
For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2025

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION AND RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
(Dollars in millions, except per share data)

Overview

Net sales for fiscal 2025 were $1,000.0 million, an increase of 4.2% from $959.7 million in fiscal 2024. Organic daily sales grew 3.1%, with the remainder contributed by the acquisition of Northline Fasteners in the second quarter.

Segment results

Industrial segment net sales were $620.0 million in fiscal 2025, up 5.8%, driven by MRO demand from manufacturing customers. Construction segment net sales were $380.0 million, up 1.7%, as non-residential activity softened in the second half.

Gross margin

Gross margin was 30.0% of net sales in fiscal 2025 compared with 29.6% in fiscal 2024. The improvement reflected price realisation and a favourable product mix, partly offset by a $12.0 million LIFO charge. Adjusted gross margin, excluding the LIFO charge, was 31.2%.

Operating expenses

Selling, general and administrative expenses were $200.0 million, or 20.0% of net sales, compared with 20.4% in fiscal 2024. Operating income was $100.0 million, an operating margin of 10.0%.

Working capital

Inventories were $164.4 million at December 31, 2025, compared with $150.2 million a year earlier. We ended the year with 60 days of inventory on hand measured against net sales.

Liquidity and capital resources

Capital expenditures were $20.0 million in fiscal 2025, approximately equal to depreciation and amortisation of $19.5 million. At December 31, 2025 we had total debt of $250.0 million and cash and cash equivalents of $50.0 million. The effective tax rate was 25.0%.
