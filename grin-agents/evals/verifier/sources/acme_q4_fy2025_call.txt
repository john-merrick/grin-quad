ACME Supply Corporation — Q4 and Full Year Fiscal 2025 Earnings Call
February 10, 2026

Operator: Good morning and welcome to the ACME Supply fourth quarter earnings call.

Dana Whitfield, Chief Executive Officer: Thanks, everyone. 2025 was a milestone year. We crossed $1.0 billion in sales for the first time in our history. In the fourth quarter we pushed through price increases of roughly 3% across the core catalogue, and customers absorbed them with very little attrition. Our customer retention rate on contract accounts was 94% for the year, in line with the last five years.

Sam Okafor, Chief Financial Officer: Operating margin in the fourth quarter was 10.4%, up from 9.8% a year ago, as we leveraged branch costs on higher volume.

Operator: Our first question comes from Priya Raman with Halvorsen Securities.

Priya Raman, Analyst, Halvorsen Securities: Thanks. Some of your competitors have talked about price increases of 5% this year. Are you seeing pricing of 5% in your channel, or is that too aggressive?

Dana Whitfield, Chief Executive Officer: We think about 3% is the right level for us. We'd rather protect share.
