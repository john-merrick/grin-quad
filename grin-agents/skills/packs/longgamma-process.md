The stages are Idea → Deep Dive → Watchlist → Owned → Rejected / Archived. Moving a company between stages is
Isaac's decision.

What each piece of research becomes:

- **Results and transcripts** become a Research Note with Thesis Impact (Isaac sets it) and a proposed
  Valuation Impact (never applied automatically).
- **A model change** becomes a model sync: Companies drivers (EBIT FY1, FY2 Bear/Base/Bull, FY3, Sustainable
  EBIT, Target Multiple per scenario, probabilities), plus one Valuation Log row per changed driver, linked to
  Isaac's rationale in his own words.
- **Target per scenario** = (EBIT FY2 × multiple − net debt) ÷ shares. The probabilities default to 25/50/25.
- **Risks** each carry a measurable kill criterion. Likelihood and impact are Isaac's 1–5 scores.

Isaac's calls, never an agent's:
- Direction (Long / Short) and Structural / Cyclical.
- Kill criteria.
- Model assumptions.
- Approving or rejecting proposals.
- Thesis Impact.
- Catalyst verdicts (Hit / Missed).
- Prompt promotions.

Agents draft, gather and check. Every agent-written row is tagged Author = Agent.
